Project Title:
Smartphone Market Intelligence Platform

Project Description:
This project focuses on understanding smartphone products
available in the local and regional markets.

The system should extract knowledge typically found in:
- Product specification sheets
- Online comparison tables
- Promotional catalogs
- Product images
- Retail pricing documents

Target Market:
- Egypt
- UAE

Expected Smartphone Data:
- Brand
- Model
- Storage variants
- RAM
- Processor
- Camera specifications
- Battery capacity
- Market price
- Currency

Important Notes:
Smartphone information is often inconsistent across documents.
Some prices are mentioned in marketing materials,
while specifications appear in separate technical sheets.

The system should handle missing or partial data gracefully
and produce structured records for database storage.
