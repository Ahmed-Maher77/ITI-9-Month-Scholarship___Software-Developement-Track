Project Title:
SmartCar Advisor Platform

Project Type:
Decision Support & Recommendation Platform (NOT a car manufacturer)

Project Description:
SmartCar Advisor is a software-based web and mobile platform.
It does NOT manufacture cars and does NOT design vehicles.

The platform helps users DECIDE which existing cars in the market
are most suitable for them.

Users do NOT buy cars from the platform.
They use it only for analysis, comparison, and decision support.

Core Purpose:
Help users choose the right car by analyzing existing market data.

What the system DOES:
- Analyze existing car models already available in different countries
- Compare prices between countries
- Compare fuel vs electric vs hybrid cars
- Compare maintenance cost, fuel cost, and resale value
- Show safety ratings, reliability, and ownership cost
- Adapt recommendations based on country regulations and infrastructure

What the system DOES NOT do:
- Does NOT manufacture cars
- Does NOT design vehicles
- Does NOT build hardware
- Does NOT produce batteries or engines

User Inputs:
- Budget range
- Country or countries of interest
- Usage purpose (daily commute, family, ride-sharing, travel)
- Fuel preference
- Environmental concerns

Target Users:
- Individual buyers
- Families
- Ride-sharing drivers
- People relocating between countries
- First-time car buyers

Global Scope:
The platform works across multiple countries and markets
and adapts recommendations based on regional data.

The project focuses on INFORMATION, ANALYSIS, and DECISION SUPPORT only.
