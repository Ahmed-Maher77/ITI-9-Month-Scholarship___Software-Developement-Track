## 📧 Email Writing in the Workplace

### Email Recipients
- **To:** Individuals required to take action
- **Cc:** Individuals who need to be informed
- **Bcc:** Recipients hidden from other addressees

### Best Practices
- Use a clear, self-explanatory subject line
- Start with the main point
- Focus on one topic per email
- Use short paragraphs and bullet points
- Maintain a polite and professional tone

**Tone Example:**
- ✔️ *“I would appreciate your support with…”*  
- ❌ *“You didn’t send the file.”*

## 📄 Report Writing Essentials
Professional reports require clarity, objectivity, and evidence-based communication:

- Use formal, fact-based language
- Support claims with data and visual elements
- Follow a clear and logical structure:
  1. Cover Page  
  2. Table of Contents  
  3. Executive Summary  
  4. Introduction  
  5. Body (Pyramid Structure)  
  6. Recommendations  

---

## ⚖️ Ethics & Professional Integrity
Ethical writing is non-negotiable in professional environments:

- Plagiarism is unethical and unacceptable
- Paraphrasing does not eliminate the need for proper attribution

---

## 🤖 Generative AI in Business Writing
Generative AI can be a powerful support tool for:
- Brainstorming ideas
- Creating outlines
- Summarizing content
- Refining language and tone

### Risks to Consider
- Inaccurate or biased outputs
- Data privacy and confidentiality concerns

📌 **Key Principle:**  
Final responsibility for content always lies with the **human user**.

---

## 🧩 Prompt Engineering Basics
Effective use of AI tools requires clear instructions:

- Be specific in requests
- Provide sufficient context
- Define the desired output format
- Break complex tasks into smaller, manageable steps

---

## ✅ Key Takeaways
- Professional writing directly reflects credibility and professionalism
- Ethical standards matter more than speed
- AI is a **tool**, not an author
