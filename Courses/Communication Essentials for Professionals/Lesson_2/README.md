## 🔹 Types of Communication

### 1. Intrapersonal Communication
- The internal dialogue you have with yourself before speaking or writing  
- Clear thinking leads to clear, confident messages

### 2. Interpersonal Communication
- The exchange of messages between two or more people  
- Forms the foundation of teamwork, collaboration, and leadership

### 3. Organizational Communication
- All formal and informal communication within organizations  
- Flows vertically (top-down and bottom-up) and horizontally across teams

---

## 🔹 Communication Channels & Forms

### Oral Communication
**Examples:** Meetings, phone calls, presentations  
- ✔ Fast and adaptive  
- ✖ Can be misinterpreted or forgotten  
- **Best Practice:** Always follow up with a written summary

### Written Communication
**Examples:** Emails, reports, instant messages  
- ✔ Provides a clear record and reduces ambiguity  
- ✖ Can be time-consuming and easier to overlook

### Non-Verbal Communication
- Includes body language, tone of voice, facial expressions, appearance, and silence  
- Often reinforces—or contradicts—spoken and written messages

---

## 🚧 Barriers to Communication
Common obstacles discussed in this lesson include:
- Cultural differences
- Psychological states (emotions, defensiveness, stress)
- Excessive use of jargon and acronyms
- Information overload
- Biases, stereotypes, and distractions

---

## 🧠 Practical Activity: Communication Management Use Cases
Participants were given real-life workplace scenarios and asked to act as **Communication Managers**:
- Identify what went wrong
- Analyze the communication barriers involved
- Propose improved alternatives using appropriate channels, tone, and structure

**Key Insight:**  
Effective communication is a learnable skill—not a matter of luck.

---

## 📏 The 7 Cs of Effective Communication
A professional standard for evaluating communication quality:

- **Clear**
- **Correct**
- **Complete**
- **Concrete**
- **Coherent**
- **Courteous**
- **Concise**

These principles apply daily to emails, meetings, presentations, and decision-making.

---

## ✅ Key Takeaways
- Choose the **right channel**, not just the fastest one
- Clarity and respect are essential for preventing conflict
- Communication failures are often **system issues**, not individual faults
