## 📘 Lesson Overview
Busy professionals rarely read documents word by word—they **scan**.

This session examined how layout, structure, typography, and writing style determine whether a message is understood quickly or ignored entirely.

---

## 🧱 Layout & White Space
Effective layout improves readability and reduces cognitive load:

- Paragraphs should not exceed **6–8 lines**
- Use:
  - Single spacing within paragraphs
  - Double spacing between paragraphs
- Keep the **right margin ragged (left-aligned)** rather than fully justified for easier scanning

---

## 🗂️ Information Hierarchy
Clear hierarchy helps readers navigate content efficiently:

- **Headings and subheadings**:
  - Organize content
  - Guide the reader through the document
  - Break long text into logical sections
- **Headers and footers**:
  - Unify the document
  - Support navigation and orientation

---

## 🎨 Typography & Visual Elements
Typography and visuals should enhance clarity, not distract:

- Black is preferred for body text
- Use color sparingly for:
  - Headings
  - Charts and graphs
- Effective formatting tools include:
  - **Bold** for emphasis
  - Bulleted lists for clarity
  - Numbered lists for sequences or steps
  - *Italics* for emphasis or titles
- ❌ Avoid **ALL CAPS** — readers often perceive it as shouting

---

## ✍️ Language & Style Principles
Clear writing depends on disciplined language choices:

- **Parallelism**: Maintain consistent grammatical structure
- **Positive voice**: Focus on what *can* be done
- **Simple word choice**:
  - Use “*use*” instead of “*utilize*”
- **Editing matters**:
  - Step away from drafts before revising
  - Seek feedback prior to finalizing documents

---

## 📊 Activity: Choosing the Right Data Visualization
Participants analyzed datasets and visual examples to:
- Select the most appropriate chart type for each dataset
- Identify the clearest way to communicate insights

**Key Insight:**  
Visuals should **clarify meaning**, not merely decorate the document.

---

## ✅ Key Takeaways
- Design supports meaning and comprehension
- Simplicity enhances credibility
- Well-chosen visuals accelerate understanding
