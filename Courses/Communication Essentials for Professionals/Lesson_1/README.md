## 📘 Lesson Overview
Communication is not merely about sending information—it is about **ensuring that meaning is transferred and understood**.

In professional environments, misunderstandings often occur not because messages are unclear, but because **context, assumptions, and interpretation** differ between individuals.

---

## 🔹 Key Concepts Covered

### 1. Communication Definition
- Communication is the **transfer and understanding of meaning**.
- **Understanding does not imply agreement**, a critical distinction in professional and workplace communication.

### 2. The Basic Communication Process
The communication cycle consists of the following elements:

Sender → Encoding → Message → Medium → Receiver → Decoding


This process is continuously influenced by:
- **Noise** (physical, psychological, cultural, emotional, etc.)
- **Feedback**, which confirms whether the message was understood as intended

### 3. Feedback as a Professional Responsibility
Effective communication requires intentional feedback, including:
- Acknowledging receipt of the message
- Providing clear, objective, and action-oriented responses
- Responding directly and in a timely manner

---

## 🧠 Interactive Learning Activities

### 1. Two-Minute Self Introductions
Participants introduced themselves briefly, highlighting how:
- Clarity
- Confidence
- Structure  
directly influence first impressions in professional settings.

### 2. Interpreting an Ambiguous Image
An unclear image was presented and participants explained what they perceived.
- Each interpretation differed
- Demonstrated how **background, experience, and context** shape meaning

### 3. Team Story Exercise — Context in Action
- Each team wrote **2.5 lines of a story**
- The paper was folded so only the **last half-line** was visible to the next team
- Resulted in humorous but fragmented stories

**Key Lesson:**  
Missing context can completely break communication—even when everyone is trying to contribute correctly.

---

## 💡 Real-World Insight
In meetings, emails, and cross-team collaboration:
- Assuming others “understand what we mean” is risky
- Without shared context, even well-intended messages can fail

---

## ✅ Key Takeaways
- Meaning is not conveyed by words alone—**context completes the message**
- Feedback is **essential**, not optional
- Effective communication begins **before** speaking or writing
