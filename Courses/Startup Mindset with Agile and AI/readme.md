# 🚀 From Idea to MVP in 3 Days – My Startup Mindset Workshop Experience

As part of my ITI 9-Month Journey, I completed an intensive workshop:

**“Startup Mindset with Agile and AI.”**

In just three days, we went from zero idea → validated MVP, using real-world methodologies and AI-powered workflows.

---

## 🎯 What We Learned & Applied

### 🔹 Lean Startup Methodology

- Defining problem statements
- Validating assumptions
- Building personas
- Creating minimal solutions
- Gathering feedback and iterating fast

### 🔹 Scrum Agile in Practice

- Product backlog (Epics & User Stories)
- Estimation with story points
- Sprint planning and execution
- Daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Real role simulation: Product Owner, Scrum Master, Developer

### 🔹 AI-Driven Workflow

We integrated AI into every stage—idea validation, documentation, task generation, and automation.

## Tools We Used

- ChatGPT & Claude – ideation and content generation
- Gamma & Jira – pitch decks and Scrum management
- Zoom AI – meeting summaries
- Make, Zapier & Lovable – rapid MVP building

---

## 💡 Our Team Project: GuideMe

We built an MVP called GuideMe:

A web platform that connects fresh graduates and career switchers in Egypt with verified experts for personalized career guidance.

### The goal:

👉 Validate demand for fast, trusted, one-on-one career consultation sessions.
