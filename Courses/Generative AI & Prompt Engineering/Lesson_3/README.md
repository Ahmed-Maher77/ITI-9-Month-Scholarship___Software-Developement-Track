---

## 🧩 Key Generative AI Terms (Simplified)

To use AI effectively, it is important to understand the language behind it:

- **LLM (Large Language Model):**  
  An AI model trained on massive text data to understand and generate human-like language.
- **AI Model:**  
  A system trained to recognize patterns, make predictions, and generate outputs based on data.
- **Transformer:**  
  The deep-learning architecture that allows models to understand context and relationships between words.
- **Tokens:**  
  Small units of text (words or parts of words) that models process.  
  ~1,000 tokens ≈ 750 words.
- **Prompt & Completion:**  
  A prompt is what you ask the AI; the completion is the response it generates.

---

## 🔍 Advanced GenAI Concepts (Without the Hype)

- **Multimodal Models:**  
  Work with text, images, audio, and more — not just one data type.
- **Reasoning Models:**  
  Designed to solve complex problems step-by-step (e.g., math, coding, logic).
- **AI Agents:**  
  Autonomous systems that can plan, decide, and act to achieve goals.
- **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation):**  
  Combines AI generation with external data sources to produce more accurate and up-to-date answers.

---

## 🌍 Real-World Examples

- Company knowledge chatbots using RAG
- AI assistants that analyze data and take actions as agents
- Multimodal tools that accept text + images in a single prompt

---

## 🧠 Key Takeaways

- Understanding AI terms = better AI usage.
- Advanced concepts like RAG and agents power real products today.
- You do not need to build models to build value with AI.
