## 🧠 Advanced Prompt Engineering Techniques

Beyond basic prompting, structured approaches help AI deliver higher-quality results:

- **RTF (Role – Task – Format):**  
  Define who the AI is, what it should do, and how the output should look.

- **RISEN:**  
  Break complex tasks into Role, Instructions, Steps, End goal, and Constraints.

- **Rephrase & Respond (RaR):**  
  Let the model rephrase the prompt first, then generate a clearer response.

- **Emotion Prompting:**  
  Adding motivational or emotional cues to improve focus and reasoning.

---

## 🔐 Responsible AI Usage & Limitations

AI is powerful, but not perfect:

- Avoid sharing personal or confidential data
- Always review AI-generated outputs
- Be aware of biases and hallucinations
- Use AI to assist, not replace, your own knowledge
- Read tools’ privacy policies carefully
