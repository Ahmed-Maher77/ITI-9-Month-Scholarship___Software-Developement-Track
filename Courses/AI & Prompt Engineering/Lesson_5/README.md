## Lesson_5: Fundamentals of Prompt Engineering

---

## 🎯 What is Prompt Engineering?

Prompt engineering is the art and science of crafting instructions that guide AI models to produce desired outputs. It bridges human intent and AI capability.

---

## 💡 Core Prompting Principles

Master these foundational concepts for better AI interactions:
• **Clarity**: Be specific about what you want<br/>
• **Context**: Provide relevant background information<br/>
• **Examples**: Show the AI what good looks like (few-shot prompting)<br/>
• **Instructions**: Give step-by-step guidance when needed<br/>
• **Iteration**: Refine your prompts based on outputs<br/>

---

## 🔑 Basic Prompting Techniques

### Zero-Shot Prompting

Ask a direct question or give a direct instruction without examples.

### Few-Shot Prompting

Provide one or more examples before requesting the final task.

### Chain-of-Thought Prompting

Ask the model to "think step by step" for better reasoning.

### System Prompts

Define the AI's role and behavior at the beginning of a conversation.

---

## ⚙️ Prompt Structure Best Practices

• **Start with context**: Help the model understand the scenario<br/>
• **Define the role**: Tell the AI what perspective to take<br/>
• **State the task clearly**: What exactly do you need?<br/>
• **Specify output format**: How should the response be structured?<br/>
• **Add constraints**: Any limitations or requirements to follow<br/>

---

## 🚀 Common Use Cases

• Answering questions and research<br/>
• Writing and content creation<br/>
• Code generation and debugging<br/>
• Brainstorming and ideation<br/>
• Learning and tutoring<br/>

---

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

• Being too vague or ambiguous<br/>
• Asking multiple unrelated questions at once<br/>
• Not providing enough context<br/>
• Expecting AI to know your specific preferences without stating them<br/>
• Trusting outputs without verification<br/>

---

## 🧠 Key Takeaways

• Good prompts lead to better AI outputs<br/>
• Clarity and context are your best friends<br/>
• Iteration helps refine results to meet your needs<br/>
• Different tasks need different prompting strategies<br/>
