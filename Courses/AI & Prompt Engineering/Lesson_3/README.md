---

## 🧩 Key Generative AI Terms (Simplified)

To use AI effectively, it's important to understand the language behind it:

• **LLM (Large Language Model):**<br/>
An AI model trained on massive text data to understand and generate human-like language.<br/>

• **AI Model:**<br/>
A system trained to recognize patterns, make predictions, and generate outputs based on data.<br/>

• **Transformer:**<br/>
The deep-learning architecture that allows models to understand context and relationships between words.<br/>

• **Tokens:**<br/>
Small units of text (words or parts of words) that models process.<br/>
~1,000 tokens ≈ 750 words.<br/>

• **Prompt & Completion:**<br/>
A prompt is what you ask the AI; the completion is the response it generates.<br/>

---

## 🔍 Advanced GenAI Concepts (Without the Hype)

• **Multimodal Models:**<br/>
Work with text, images, audio, and more — not just one data type.<br/>

• **Reasoning Models:**<br/>
Designed to solve complex problems step-by-step (e.g., math, coding, logic).<br/>

• **AI Agents:**<br/>
Autonomous systems that can plan, decide, and act to achieve goals.<br/>

• **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation):**<br/>
Combines AI generation with external data sources to produce more accurate and up-to-date answers.<br/>

---

## 🌍 Real-World Examples

• Company knowledge chatbots using RAG<br/>
• AI assistants that analyze data and take actions as agents<br/>
• Multimodal tools that accept text + images in a single prompt<br/>

---

## 🧠 Key Takeaways

• Understanding AI terms = better AI usage<br/>
• Advanced concepts like RAG and agents power real products today<br/>
• You don't need to build models to build value with AI<br/>
