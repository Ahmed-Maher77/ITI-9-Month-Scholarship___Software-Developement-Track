## 🧠 Advanced Prompt Engineering Techniques

Beyond basic prompting, structured approaches help AI deliver higher-quality results:<br/>
• RTF (Role – Task – Format):<br/>
Define who the AI is, what it should do, and how the output should look.<br/>

• RISEN:<br/>
Break complex tasks into Role, Instructions, Steps, End goal, and Constraints.<br/>

• Rephrase & Respond (RaR):<br/>
Let the model rephrase the prompt first, then generate a clearer response.<br/>

• Emotion Prompting:<br/>
Adding motivational or emotional cues to improve focus and reasoning.<br/>

---

## 🔐 Responsible AI Usage & Limitations

AI is powerful, but not perfect:<br/>
• Avoid sharing personal or confidential data<br/>
• Always review AI-generated outputs<br/>
• Be aware of biases and hallucinations<br/>
• Use AI to assist, not replace, your own knowledge<br/>
• Read tools' privacy policies carefully<br/>
