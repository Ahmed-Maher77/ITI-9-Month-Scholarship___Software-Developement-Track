# Lesson_4: Agile Methodology in Action

Agile is the dominant methodology in modern software development.  
In this lesson, we explored Agile values, principles, workflows, and why it empowers fast-moving, customer-focused teams.

---

## 🔹 The 4 Agile Manifesto Values

1. **Individuals & Interactions** > Processes & Tools  
2. **Working Software** > Comprehensive Documentation  
3. **Customer Collaboration** > Contract Negotiation  
4. **Responding to Change** > Following a Plan  

These values emphasize:  
➡️ *People, feedback, collaboration, and adaptability are more important than rigid processes.*

---

## 🔹 Agile Workflow

**Backlog → Sprint Planning → Development → Testing → Review → Deployment → Feedback → Next Sprint**

### Core Agile Elements
- User stories (e.g., *“As a user, I want…”*)  
- Sprint Planning  
- Daily Standups  
- Continuous testing within the sprint  
- CI/CD pipelines  
- Sprint Review & Retrospective  

Agile focuses on delivering value **incrementally** while continuously improving the product and the process.

---

## 🔹 The 12 Agile Principles (Highlights)

✔ Deliver valuable software frequently  
✔ Welcome changing requirements  
✔ Communicate face-to-face  
✔ Working software is the measure of progress  
✔ Maintain a sustainable development pace  
✔ Keep things simple  
✔ Build self-organizing teams  
✔ Reflect and adapt regularly  

These principles shape Agile teams into more productive, collaborative, and adaptive units.

---

## 🔹 Real-World Agile Examples

- **Facebook** ships updates daily  
- **Spotify squads** work in rapid iterative cycles  
- **Amazon** deploys *hundreds* of times per day  

All enabled by the combination of **Agile + DevOps**.

---

## ⭐ Key Takeaways

- Agile is **a mindset**, not just a framework.  
- Continuous feedback ensures teams build the *right* product.  
- Collaboration, adaptability, and iteration drive success.
