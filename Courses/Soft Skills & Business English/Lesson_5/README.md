🎓 **Business English Course — ITI 9-Month Journey**  
#Lesson_5: **Critical Thinking, Business Decisions & Consumer Behavior**

---

## **Intro**
Today’s session focused on developing our analytical thinking, decision-making skills, and business communication — all through real-world scenarios and interactive activities that reflect everyday workplace challenges.

---

## 🔍 **1. Success Story Analysis — Critical & Direct Thinking**
We began by watching a 25-minute video presenting a real success story of a team that overcame challenges to achieve a major breakthrough.  

Our trainer asked two types of questions:

- **Direct questions** → understanding events, timeline, and actions  
- **Critical thinking questions** → analyzing decisions, identifying mistakes, evaluating alternatives  

**Example questions we discussed:**  
- Why did the team succeed?  
- What strategies made the difference?  
- What would you have done differently as the leader?  

This helped us practice **business reasoning**, not just listening.

---

## 💼 **2. Investment Planning Activity — Decision Making in Business**

We worked on a scenario where a company needed to build an investment plan **not exceeding $3 million**.

The task included:
- A table of estimated costs for various investment options  
- An audio explaining the company’s challenges and priorities  

Each group presented their investment plan and justified:
- Why the chosen options were selected  
- How they solve the company’s problems  
- How the plan fits within the budget  
- Expected impact or return  

This activity enhanced our:
- ✔ Business data analysis  
- ✔ Teamwork & discussions  
- ✔ Clear decision presentation  
- ✔ Logical justification skills  

---

## 🛍️ **3. Shopping & Consumer Behavior**

We explored:
- Online vs offline shopping  
- Personal buying habits  
- Factors that influence customer choices  

Then we listened to **three audios**, each person explaining their preferred shopping method and the reasons behind it.  
This improved our **listening, comparison, and summarizing skills**.

---

## 🎧 **4. Listening Practice — Real People, Real Opinions**

We listened to three recordings where individuals explained:
- Their preferred shopping method  
- Why they prefer it  
- How it matches their lifestyle  

We practiced:
- Extracting main ideas  
- Identifying supporting details  
- Differentiating facts from opinions  

Strengthening our **Business English listening comprehension**.

---

## 📌 **Assignments**

### **1️⃣ Presentation: Inspiring Female Leader**
We were asked to prepare a presentation about a woman role model in a leadership position, covering:
- Early life & upbringing  
- Education  
- Career path  
- Achievements  
- Leadership qualities  
- Impact & legacy  

This developed our research, presentation, and storytelling abilities.

### **2️⃣ Reading & Critical Thinking Exercise**
We read a set of rules and determined whether each statement was **True or False**, with explanations.

This improved our:
- Information analysis  
- Understanding of formal instructions  
- Clarity in communication  

---

## ⭐ **Key Takeaways**
- Critical thinking is essential for business success  
- Effective decisions require data + analysis + justification  
- Understanding customer behavior enhances sales & communication  
- Presentations build professional confidence  
- Listening practice strengthens real-world business communication
