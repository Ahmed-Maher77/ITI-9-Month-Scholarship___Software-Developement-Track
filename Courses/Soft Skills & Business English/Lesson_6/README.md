### Lesson Overview

This lesson focused on the role of **storytelling, leadership, and effective communication** in professional and real-world contexts. Through discussions, presentations, and feedback, we explored how successful leaders and brands use stories to inspire, influence, and build long-term impact.

---

### Session Content

#### 1. Leadership Through Storytelling
- Watched a video highlighting the journey of **Colonel Sanders**, founder of KFC.
- Analyzed his path from repeated failures to global success.
- Discussed how persistence, belief in one’s vision, and consistency contribute to sustainable success.

#### 2. Interactive Discussion
- Students retold the story from their own perspectives, focusing on personal takeaways.
- One participant facilitated a group discussion by asking:
  - What lessons were learned?
  - What was the “recipe” behind success?
  - Why did the brand succeed and last?
- Shared similar real-life stories of leaders who turned failure into success.

#### 3. Presentation Practice: Female Role Models in Leadership
- Each student delivered a presentation on a **female leadership role model**.
- Presentations covered:
  - Background and leadership journey
  - Key achievements
  - Leadership style and global impact
- Emphasis was placed on clarity, structure, confidence, and storytelling.

#### 4. Feedback & Evaluation
- The mentor provided detailed feedback for each presentation, highlighting:
  - Strengths in content and delivery
  - Areas for improvement such as engagement, flow, and professional tone
- Feedback was framed as a tool for continuous improvement and professional growth.

---

### Learning Outcomes

By the end of this lesson, students were able to:
- Understand the importance of storytelling in leadership and branding
- Communicate ideas clearly and confidently in a professional setting
- Analyze leadership journeys and extract practical lessons
- Deliver structured presentations and respond to constructive feedback
- Recognize failure as a key component of success
