## What Is Marketing?

Marketing is not just selling.

Marketing is the process of:

- Understanding customer needs
- Promoting a product or service
- Encouraging a specific action (purchase, sign-up, etc.)

Important clarification: marketing does not create needs. Instead, it creates demand by connecting existing needs with the right solution.

## Marketing vs. Sales

Many people confuse marketing and sales, but they serve different purposes:

- Marketing: builds awareness, interest, and desire
- Sales: converts that desire into actual purchases

Simply put: marketing brings the customer, and sales closes the deal.

## Types of Marketing

### 1. Traditional Marketing

- TV, radio, billboards, flyers
- Expensive
- Less targeted
- Hard to measure ROI

### 2. Digital Marketing

- Social media, websites, email, search engines
- Cost-effective
- Highly targeted
- Measurable through analytics and data

Correction: digital marketing is not always one-way communication. It can also be two-way through comments, messages, and engagement.

## Before Starting Any Project

Before launching any product, we must:

- Define project scope and objectives
- Conduct market research
- Plan technical requirements
- Build a marketing strategy

Without this foundation, decisions become guesses instead of data-driven actions.

## Why Market Research Is Critical

Market research helps us:

- Validate ideas before building
- Understand customer behavior
- Analyze competitors
- Discover opportunities and threats

When do we use it?

- New idea: validate demand
- Existing business: stay competitive and updated

## Competitor Types

- Direct competitors: same product (example: Coca-Cola vs. Pepsi)
- Indirect competitors: different product, same need (example: pizza vs. burger)
- Potential competitors: future market entrants

## Common Market Research Mistakes

- Starting with a biased conclusion
- Targeting a limited or non-representative audience
- Asking wrong or insufficient questions
- Treating research as a one-time task

Market research is a continuous process, not a one-time activity.

## Understanding Customers

### Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs

Customers buy based on different levels of needs:

- Basic (food, safety)
- Psychological (belonging, esteem)
- Self-fulfillment

### The Golden Circle (Start With Why)

- Why: purpose
- How: process
- What: product

Strong brands communicate the "Why" first.

## Key Takeaways

- Marketing is not sales
- Marketing creates demand, not needs
- Research is the foundation of any successful project
- Understanding customers is more important than pushing products
- Strategy always comes before execution
