## Lesson 4: Marketing Plan and SOSTAC Model

---

## 🔹 What Is a Marketing Plan?

A Marketing Plan is a clear roadmap that outlines:

- Your goals
- Your target audience
- Your strategy and tactics
- How you will measure success

📌 It is not just ideas; it is execution with direction.

---

## 🔹 Why Is It Important?

- Keeps your team aligned
- Reduces random decisions
- Helps measure performance
- Increases chances of success

💡 Without a plan, marketing becomes guessing.

---

## 🔹 SOSTAC Model

A powerful framework to build a complete marketing plan:

### 1. Situation Analysis

👉 Where are we now?

- Market overview
- Competitors
- Customer insights
- SWOT (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats)

### 2. Objectives

👉 Where do we want to go?

Objectives should be:

- Clear
- Measurable
- Time-bound

💡 **Example:**
"Increase website traffic by 30% in 3 months."

### 3. Strategy

👉 How will we get there?

- Target audience
- Positioning
- Channels (social media, SEO, ads)

### 4. Tactics

👉 What exactly will we do?

- Content plan
- Ad campaigns
- Platforms (Facebook, Instagram, etc.)

📌 Strategy = big picture
📌 Tactics = detailed actions

### 5. Action

👉 Who does what?

- Roles and responsibilities
- Timeline
- Tools and resources

### 6. Control

👉 How do we measure success?

- KPIs (traffic, conversions, sales)
- Analytics tools
- Performance tracking

💡 What gets measured gets improved.

---

## 🔹 Real Insight 💡

A good marketing plan is:

- Flexible (adapts to change)
- Data-driven
- Customer-focused

---

## 🔹 Key Takeaways

- Marketing Plan is your roadmap for success
- SOSTAC gives a structured approach
- Always measure and optimize
- Execution is as important as planning
