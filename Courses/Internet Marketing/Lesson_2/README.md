# 🎓 Internet Marketing — ITI Journey

## Lesson 2: STP Model (Segmentation, Targeting, Positioning)

---

## 🔹 What Is STP?

The STP Model is one of the most important frameworks in marketing:

- **Segmentation** → Divide the market
- **Targeting** → Choose your audience
- **Positioning** → Define how you want to be perceived

📌 You can’t market to everyone, and you shouldn’t.

---

## 🔹 1. Segmentation

Segmentation means dividing the market into smaller groups based on shared characteristics.

### Common Types

- **Demographic** → age, gender, income
- **Geographic** → country, city
- **Behavioral** → buying habits, usage
- **Psychographic** → lifestyle, interests

💡 **Example:**
A fitness app may target:

- Young professionals (demographic)
- Living in cities (geographic)
- Interested in a healthy lifestyle (psychographic)

---

## 🔹 2. Targeting

After segmentation, we choose the most valuable segment.

### Based On

- Market size
- Profitability
- Accessibility

### Targeting Strategies

- **Niche Marketing** → focus on a specific segment (e.g., vegan products)
- **Mass Marketing** → target everyone (e.g., soft drinks)
- **Segmented Marketing** → different offers for different segments

---

## 🔹 3. Positioning

Positioning is how your product is perceived in the customer’s mind.

💡 It answers:
👉 "Why should customers choose you?"

### Examples

When we think of:

- **Luxury** → Mercedes / BMW
- **Fast food** → McDonald's

📌 Strong positioning = clear, consistent, and memorable.

---

## 🔹 Important Insight 💡

Not everyone buys your product.

👉 "What do all people buy?"
Nothing.

That’s why targeting is critical.

---

## 🔹 Key Takeaways

- ✔️ Don’t target everyone — be specific
- ✔️ Choose segments based on real value
- ✔️ Position your product clearly in the market
- ✔️ Perception is just as important as the product itself
